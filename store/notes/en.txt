A Discord button, in the popup between Clear cache and Options and at the end of the Options tabs, opens the extension's server: ask for help, report a bad translation, suggest an idea. The About tab also links to Twitch Chat Translator, the same extension for Twitch.

Fewer chat words are read as the wrong language: Hinglish, romanised Japanese and Russian, laughter inside an English sentence and more. On a new set of 500 real chat lines, wrong confident answers went from 17 to 6. One-word Chinese, Japanese and Korean replies are now translated, while laughter, scores and ratings are still skipped.

Fixes: after one line went to a backup engine, every later line followed it; your engine order now comes back after 60 seconds. Numbers stay right in cached translations ("100 subs" and "1000 subs" no longer share an entry). Built-in translations work with punctuation and stretched words ("holaaa", "merci!"). With on-device first, a line the on-device engine hands back unchanged now goes to the cloud instead of being dropped. DeepL now receives the source language of a batch when it is known.
