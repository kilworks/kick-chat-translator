The settings and the popup were reworked to be plain and consistent. The settings now open on Display, where the language and the look are. Every on/off setting is a switch that applies at once. Font, density, chat theme and accent colour show their choices as buttons and named colour swatches, and the style preview stays in view while you change them.

Each translation engine now says its state in words: Ready, or Paused with the reason and when it comes back. The DeepL card appears once DeepL is in the order or a key is saved, and the key stays hidden until you show it. The popup shows one main button at a time, puts the version in the footer, and fits without a scrollbar in every interface language.

Fixes: a channel your channel lists leave out now says "Not translated here" in the chat bar instead of going quiet, and the popup offers a way to Filters. Engine settings can no longer contradict each other. Glossary lines written with "->" or "=>" work, and lines the glossary cannot read are listed under it.
