Lighter on fast chats. Measured on a chat running at 20 to 25 messages a second, against 3.6.1: the work the extension adds to the page drops by 30 %, page re-layouts by 60 % and style recalculations by 55 %. The "…" mark now only shows on a line still waiting for its translation after a quarter of a second, about 145 times a minute instead of 780.

A translation engine that stops answering no longer holds its lines: after 8 seconds (12 for a batch) the next engine takes over, where 3.6.1 waited forever. A line still unanswered after 20 seconds shows its retry button.

Fixes for long sessions: the chat bar, the chat lines and the rate-limit hourglass no longer pile up hidden listeners and timers over hours of watching.
