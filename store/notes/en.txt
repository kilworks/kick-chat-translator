New option, off by default: translate a channel only after you switch it on. Turn on "Translate a channel only after I switch it on in the bar" in the Display tab. Every channel then stays untranslated until you press play on the bar, or Resume in the popup, and the choice lasts for that tab only.

Chinese and Japanese chat reads better. Short lines written only with Han characters are no longer hidden or mistaken for the other language, Taiwanese and mainland Chinese are told apart far more often, and a mostly Cantonese chat is recognised, so its lines are translated instead of skipped.

Safer on Chrome: your settings and your DeepL key are now closed to the scripts that run inside Kick pages, and importing a settings file can no longer change your usage statistics choice, your MyMemory email or your Lingva address.

Failed lines say "Translation failed" in your language instead of a code, and a translation service that is cooling down is no longer asked again on every batch. Notices are announced to screen readers and shown with reduced motion on.

Lighter on fast chats: translated lines are found without a heavy style search, a hidden paused tab is no longer read, and the background worker writes the cache once per batch.

The About tab now explains the research behind the translator and links the public repositories.
