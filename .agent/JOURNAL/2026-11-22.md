# 2026-11-22, fast chat performance, phases 0 to 3

Branch `feat/perf-fast-chat` from `072aac5` (3.6.1). Symptom reported: on the
fastest Kick chats a lag builds up over a session until a reload.

## Measured

- Offline, release 3.6.1, fake engine 300 +- 200 ms, `--unique 0.6`, 1000-row
  page: S1 (20 msg/s, 30 min), S2 (25 msg/s, 15 min), S3 (429 bursts, 20 min),
  S4 (10 % hung calls, 15 min), S5 (recycled rows, 20 min). With the harness
  counter fixed, every pass drift green: heap settles at about 10.3 MB from
  minute 7, nodes and listeners flat.
- Overhead over the page without the extension, S1: script +5.2 ms/s, tasks
  +29.5 ms/s, layouts +25.6/s, style recalcs +28.4/s. Most of it is DOM writes.
- The channel budget (200 a minute) refuses 66 to 73 % of sends on S1 to S5.
- Sends per distinct row 1.001 to 1.002 (M1 refuted). `dom.row.seen` 2.35 per
  message on S2 (M4 confirmed).
- S4 on 3.6.1: 344 hung Google calls, 0 handed to MyMemory (no deadline, R1).
- Live, kick.com, anonymous profile, busiest of five listed channels, target
  French. Kick's chat arrives over its realtime service, pushes batched one per
  line in a frame; rows carry no message id and `data-index` is renumbered once
  the list is full. Matching frame to row on name and text: chat lag p50 about
  270 ms, p95 about 510 ms, flat over 20 min, with or without the extension.
- Live CPU profiles at minutes 3 and 18: Kick's virtualizer (`resizeItem`,
  `_measureElement`) 43 to 260 ms per 30 s without the extension, 40 to 465
  with 3.6.1. The extension's own script 35 to 52 ms per 30 s. Root cause on
  Kick's side (K1), fed by every row height change.
- M5 on S2 (10 min, R only against R + M5): placeholders 780 a minute to about
  145, extra layouts 31.9/s to 12.9/s, extra style recalcs 36.1/s to 17/s,
  extra tasks 31.4 to 22.2 ms/s.
- M4 fix on top of M5: extra script 6.0 against 6.1 ms/s, tasks 22.2 against
  22.7 ms/s. No gain, not kept.

## Failed tries and wrong probes

- First S1 read red on heap (+10.3 %, 9.46 to 11.63 MB). A heap snapshot diff
  (+12280 strings, a growing Map backing store) named the harness: its send
  counter kept every text in the extension's world. Fixed, re-measured flat.
  Passes run with the old counter (P0-S1, P0-S2, P0-S2m, P0-S3) have that cost
  in their heap and script.
- Channel discovery from /browse links picked slow channels (69 lines a
  minute): links are not sorted by audience. Kick's live listing endpoint is.
- Live probe, first versions: WebSocket parsing expected Pusher events and
  matched nothing (Kick moved to a realtime service); rows keyed on
  `data-index` stopped counting after a minute (renumbering), then keyed on
  index plus content they were counted again at every renumbering (more rows
  than messages, p95 lag of 20 to 33 s from mismatched frames). Content-only
  keys gave 0 unmatched rows.
- Live B read red on cost (+60 % script) because traffic doubled and
  translations tripled during the pass; per WebSocket message the extension
  page goes 1.3 to 2.2 ms against 1.3 to 1.95 ms without.
